# Vinyl Player Mode - Quick Start Guide

## How to Enable Vinyl Mode

1. Open PixelTunes app
2. Click the **"Vinyl Player"** button at the bottom of the screen
3. The interface will switch to Vinyl Player Mode

## How to Use Vinyl Player

### 🎵 Play/Pause Music

**Method 1: Tap the Vinyl Disc**
- Single tap anywhere on the black vinyl disc
- Music starts/stops
- Needle automatically moves on/off the disc

**Method 2: Drag the Needle Arm**
- Grab the needle arm at the top-right of the vinyl
- Drag DOWN to place on vinyl → Music plays ▶️
- Drag UP to lift off vinyl → Music pauses ⏸️
- Needle snaps to position when you release

**Method 3: Use Control Buttons**
- Click ◄◄ Previous or ►►  Next buttons below the vinyl

### 🎚️ Seek Through Song

**Rotate the Vinyl Disc**
- Touch the vinyl and rotate clockwise → Seek forward
- Touch the vinyl and rotate counter-clockwise → Seek backward
- Audio pitch changes as you rotate (scratch effect!)
- Pitch returns to normal when you stop

**Use the Progress Bar**
- Drag the progress bar on the left side
- Shows current time / total time

### 👀 View Playlist

**"Up Next" Preview**
- See the next 3 songs in your playlist
- Located on the left side below the progress bar
- Updates automatically when song changes

### 🔗 Attribution Links

**At the bottom of the screen:**
- **LinkedIn** button → Opens Navaneeth Sankar K P's LinkedIn profile
- **@navuxneeth/PixelTunes** button → Opens GitHub repository

## Cool Features

### 🎧 Pitch Adjustment
When you manually rotate the vinyl:
- **Clockwise (fast)** → Pitch goes up (chipmunk effect)
- **Counter-clockwise (slow)** → Pitch goes down (slow-mo effect)
- Music keeps playing with the adjusted pitch
- Returns to normal when you stop rotating

### 🎨 8-bit Retro Style
- VT323 pixel font for authentic retro look
- Black vinyl with realistic groove circles
- Smooth animations (300ms needle, 3s vinyl rotation)
- Consistent with PixelTunes aesthetic

### 💾 Save Your Preference
- Your mode choice (Normal vs Vinyl) is saved
- Reopening the app remembers your preference
- No need to switch modes every time

## Switching Back to Normal Mode

1. Click the **"Normal Player"** button at the bottom
2. Interface switches back to standard layout
3. All controls still work normally

## Tips & Tricks

✨ **Scratch Effect**: Quickly rotate the vinyl back and forth for a DJ scratch sound

✨ **Smooth Seeking**: Slowly rotate the vinyl to find exact moments in songs

✨ **Quick Pause**: Lift the needle instead of reaching for pause button

✨ **See What's Coming**: Check "Up Next" to plan your listening

✨ **Share the App**: Click GitHub button to share PixelTunes with friends

## Troubleshooting

**Needle won't move?**
- Make sure you're dragging vertically (up/down)
- The needle has smooth snap animation when released

**Vinyl not rotating?**
- Check if music is actually playing
- Tap the vinyl or drag needle down to start

**Pitch sounds weird?**
- This is intentional! It mimics a real turntable
- Stop touching the vinyl to return to normal pitch

**Attribution links not opening?**
- Make sure you have a web browser installed
- Check your internet connection
