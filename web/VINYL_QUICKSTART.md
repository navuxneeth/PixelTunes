# Vintage Vinyl Player - Quick Start Guide

## 🎵 What is Vinyl Player Mode?

Transform your music listening experience with a fully interactive vintage vinyl record player! Tap the disc to play, drag the needle to pause, and rotate the vinyl to seek - just like a real turntable.

## 🚀 How to Get Started

### 1. Upload Your Music

First, you need music in your player:

- Click **"Upload Files"** or **"Upload Folder"**
- Or drag and drop audio files onto the upload area
- Supported formats: MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC

### 2. Switch to Vinyl Mode

Once songs are loaded:

- Look for the **"VINYL PLAYER"** button at the bottom
- Click it to activate vintage vinyl player mode
- Your preference will be saved for next time!

## 🎛️ How to Use the Vinyl Player

### Playing Music

**Method 1: Tap the Vinyl Disc** (Easiest!)
```
👆 Click anywhere on the black vinyl disc
   → Music starts playing
   → Needle moves onto the vinyl
   → Disc rotates continuously

👆 Click again to pause
   → Music stops
   → Needle lifts off
   → Disc stops rotating
```

**Method 2: Drag the Needle Arm**
```
👆 Click and hold the wooden needle arm
↓  Drag DOWN towards the vinyl
   → Music starts playing

↑  Drag UP away from the vinyl
   → Music pauses
```

**Method 3: Use the Progress Bar**
```
Just drag the seek bar below the album art
```

### Seeking Through Songs

**Rotate the Vinyl Disc**
```
1. 👆 Click and hold on the vinyl disc
2. 🔄 Move your mouse in a circular motion
3. 🎵 Hear the pitch change as you rotate
4. 📍 Song seeks forward/backward
5. 👋 Release to return to normal playback
```

**What You'll Notice:**
- Rotating clockwise → Song moves forward + pitch goes up
- Rotating counter-clockwise → Song moves backward + pitch goes down
- Sounds like scratching a real vinyl record!

### Navigation

**Previous/Next Buttons**
```
[◄◄] Previous  - Go back to previous song
[►►] Next      - Skip to next song
```

**Playlist Preview**
```
"UP NEXT" section shows the next 3 songs
Click on any preview to see details
```

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Experience

1. **Use headphones** to hear pitch changes clearly
2. **Adjust volume first** before rotating vinyl (pitch changes can be loud!)
3. **Try all three play methods** to find your favorite
4. **Experiment with rotation** - fast spins = crazy pitch effects!

### Smooth Playback

- The vinyl rotates at 20 RPM when playing (3 seconds per revolution)
- Needle animates smoothly over 300ms
- Everything syncs automatically!

### Keyboard Shortcuts

Even in vinyl mode, these still work:

- **Space Bar**: Play/Pause
- **Left Arrow**: Seek backward 5 seconds
- **Right Arrow**: Seek forward 5 seconds

## 🌗 Themes

Toggle between light and dark themes:

- Click the **sun/moon icon** in top-right corner
- Vinyl player looks great in both!

## 📱 Mobile Support

Works on phones and tablets too!

- **Tap the vinyl** to play/pause
- **Touch and drag** the needle
- **Rotate with touch** gestures

## 🔄 Switching Back

- Click **"NORMAL PLAYER"** button at the bottom
- Your choice is saved for next visit

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**Vinyl won't spin?**
- Make sure audio is playing (click the disc!)
- Check browser console for errors

**Needle doesn't respond?**
- Click and hold, then drag vertically
- Make sure you're dragging far enough (20px threshold)

**Pitch not changing?**
- Your browser needs Web Audio API support
- Try Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge

**Mode doesn't save?**
- Enable localStorage in browser settings
- Don't use private/incognito mode
